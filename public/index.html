❓ how to play, modes & tips

controls

A–Z type the quote to drive · backspace fixes mistakes (realistic mode) · esc leaves the race · N cycles day / sunset / night · after alt-tab, click anywhere to resume.

the 7 modes

  • 🏁 classic — replay a real TypeRacer race: real quote, real opponents, bots that replay their exact keystroke timing.
  • 🕹️ single — a real race quote, solo time trial. Nobody else on track.
  • 🧘 practice — random quote from Monkeytype's public dump (cached a week — light on data). No stakes.
  • 💀 death — classic race, but your first mistake ends the run. How far can you get?
  • 🤖 bot battle — choose the number of bots and their exact WPM, then beat them on a bundled quote.
  • 🎯 drill — aim trainers: common words, numbers & symbols, or JavaScript code. Solo, works offline.
  • 📝 custom — paste any text (lyrics, homework, docs) and race through it.

ghost & daily

👻 Personal ghost — after your first full run, a toggle appears: your fastest run returns as a translucent rival in any race. Beat it and it gets faster. 📅 Daily challenge — one seeded race per day, identical for every player worldwide. Keep the 🔥 streak alive.

tips

Accuracy beats raw speed — every mistake costs a backspace trip. Chain 10+ correct keys for a 🔥 combo. In death mode, typing at ~90% of your top speed survives much longer than full send.
